# Reviewed validation cases and first observed pull

Implemented after `0bd8d52` on `codex/launch-readiness`. Version remains 0.9.5.0. This milestone is local, with no publication or tag.

## What changed

Review now explains evidence readiness for each mechanic occurrence, including windows that end or rearm without a decision. Missing statuses, baselines, observation gaps and unknown required values affect the relevant occurrence. An independently complete earlier or later window remains comparable. Uncertain competing rules remain part of a decision's evidence requirements, including an early candidate whose unknown values could have delayed it into a later conflict.

Unknown encounter scope, ambiguous cast identity, malformed observations with unknown timing, incomplete source imports and recording limits remain global barriers to successful comparison. Legacy results without occurrence metadata retain conservative handling. Live assignment and navigation behavior are unchanged.

**Saved reviewed cases** keeps independently entered expectations, a name and a review note. Saving and loading are explicit. Cases bind to the tested plan, recording, player and territory, and work under the polling/gap stress scenarios. Changed rules, boards or evidence require a new review. Cases do not enable rules or generate their own expected answer.

The versioned local library holds up to 64 cases in at most 4 MiB. Ordered background IO and atomic replacement publish only durable success. Failed writes preserve the previous library; malformed, unsupported or oversized libraries cannot be silently replaced. Disposal stops queued operations and UI publication, though an atomic write already underway may finish.

Cases retain a digest and reviewed metadata without duplicating recordings, names, source URLs or movement histories. **The original recording must be retained separately.** Replay retention can remove a recording needed by a case. Pinning recordings and self-contained replay fixtures remain follow-up work.

The supplied [fight 9](https://www.fflogs.com/reports/yaP6A3hwN7b8KnQJ?fight=9) is a Lindwurm II kill, encounter 105, lasting 536.735 seconds. It contains none of the Grotesquerie: Act 2 assignment inputs. The same report's [fight 2](https://www.fflogs.com/reports/yaP6A3hwN7b8KnQJ?fight=2) is the useful Lindwurm P1 kill, encounter 104, lasting 425.672 seconds. Metadata and events were verified through Shikari's existing authenticated FF Logs client. Credentials and token responses were not printed or saved.

The P1 assignment cast starts at **117.258s**, completes at **120.244s**, and all eight number/Bonds pairs arrive at **121.403s**: **4.145 seconds after cast start**. Recorded number removals run from 147.383s to 162.400s.

The anonymized regression fixture retains one cast and 32 relevant application/removal events. It represents eight assignment identities from **one independent pull**. Literal expected identities were checked against the observed IDs and the pinned [WTFDIG guide](https://github.com/mczub/wtfdig/blob/2262bfea410539c9d2465a03b67094bc4c1f7761/src/routes/74/m12s/data.ts#L4555), [BossMod ID declarations](https://github.com/awgil/ffxiv_bossmod/blob/ecf7a1d2d4a694bf177fc5a4b7966e0f6524f2ad/BossMod.Modules/Dawntrail/Savage/RM12S1TheLindwurm/RM12S1TheLindwurmEnums.cs) and [assignment handler](https://github.com/awgil/ffxiv_bossmod/blob/ecf7a1d2d4a694bf177fc5a4b7966e0f6524f2ad/BossMod.Modules/Dawntrail/Savage/RM12S1TheLindwurm/GrotesquerieAct2.cs).

The actual importer, timeline, passive decoder and chronological runner reproduce all eight identities, reject pre-arrival classification, respect removals and preserve unknown log fields. A four-second test window misses the statuses; a six-second **test-only** window classifies them. No production default or profile changed. A deliberately incorrect expected A/B identity failed the regression.

The fixture has empty label-only boards and a synthetic territory binding. FF Logs game-zone metadata is not treated as a verified Dalamud territory. An unverified-scope case remains unverified even when its candidate label matches. These checks establish observed assignment identity and timing, not Caro adherence, destinations, routes or individual mechanic success.
